# Sector knowledge research notes

The detailed operational checklists live in `skills/lca-sector/references/`. This document records the knowledge map needed to expand the plugin.

## Energy, fuels, hydrogen, and CCS

Know fuel-cycle boundaries, HHV/LHV, upstream methane, flaring/venting, capacity factor, plant efficiency and load, grid accounting, transmission/losses, storage, hydrogen pressure/purity/compression/liquefaction/leakage, electrolyzer stack replacement, water treatment, CCS capture penalty, transport/injection, permanence, and counterfactual. Distinguish captured fossil CO2, biogenic CCS, DAC, avoided emissions, and removals.

## Chemicals, refining, polymers, and bioprocesses

Know reaction stoichiometry, conversion/selectivity/yield, refineries and joint products, solvent/catalyst/reagent use, heat integration, separations, fugitive VOCs, flare/vent, wastewater, polymerization and additives, feedstock carbon, end-of-life pathways. Bioprocesses add media, sterilization, aeration/agitation, titer/rate/yield, seed train, downstream purification, cleaning and biological residue treatment.

## Metals, minerals, cement, and batteries

Know ore grade/recovery, beneficiation, smelting/refining, tailings/slag, reductants, direct process emissions, scrap loops, alloy grade, clinker/calcination, SCM allocation, carbonation, service performance. Batteries add active-material route, cell/pack BOM, dry-room and formation energy, yield/scrap, usable capacity, round-trip efficiency, cycle/calendar degradation, duty/temperature, replacement, second life, and recycling quality.

## Manufacturing, electronics, and semiconductors

Know BOM, fabrication yield, machining/forming/coating/heat treatment, facility/cleanroom loads, process gases and abatement, ultrapure water, die/wafer/node/yield, packaging/testing, component supply chains, use-phase workload/idle energy, lifetime/repairability, and e-waste. Generic datasets can be inappropriate for fast-changing technologies.

## Buildings and infrastructure

Know EPD/PCR modules, declared versus functional unit, reference service life, construction losses, maintenance/repair/replacement, operational energy/water, climate/occupancy, demolition and Module D or analogous separate benefits. Match EPD validity, PCR, modules, geography, and product performance.

## Transport and logistics

Know passenger/tonne-km, load/occupancy, empty returns, distance/route, vehicle class, fuel cycle, real-world operation, infrastructure/vehicle production, non-exhaust emissions, refrigeration, warehousing, marine methane slip/black carbon, aviation non-CO2, EV charging timing and battery replacement.

## Agriculture, forestry, food, and bio-based systems

Know yield, rotation, fertilizer/liming/pesticides, soil/field emissions, irrigation, machinery, land occupation/change, soil carbon, residue and manure counterfactuals, enteric/manure CH4, food/feed co-products, nutrient/edible function, cold chain/cooking, loss/waste, and biogenic carbon. Preserve uncertainty and avoid broad label-based conclusions.

## Water and wastewater

Know source/basin, withdrawal/consumption/return, treatment quality, pumping head, chemicals, membranes/media, energy, distribution loss, sludge, brine, effluent pollutant mass, wastewater CH4/N2O, biogas and nutrient recovery, receiving environment. Close hydraulic and contaminant balances.

## Waste, recycling, and circular systems

Know waste composition/moisture/contamination, collection/sorting, processing yield, rejects, secondary-material quality/demand/substitution, landfill methane/leachate/storage, incineration energy, compost/AD emissions, informal/exported fates, reuse/refurbishment, and recycling formula. Recovery rate, recycling yield, recycled content, and displacement are different quantities.

## Cross-sector modules to add later

- textiles and apparel;
- pharmaceuticals/biomedical products;
- mining-specific tailings and acid drainage;
- data centers and AI services;
- aerospace and defense constraints;
- renewable power equipment and grid storage;
- forestry products and long-lived biogenic carbon;
- packaging and food-shelf-life interactions;
- carbon dioxide removal and durable storage certification contexts.

Each future module should define functional units, process maps, direct emissions, key data sources, model equations, benchmarks, allocation/recycling conventions, future scenarios, and adversarial eval cases.
