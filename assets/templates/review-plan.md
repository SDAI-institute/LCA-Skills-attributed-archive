## Materiality and finding severity

- Critical: invalidates intended use or claim.
- Major: can materially change results/conclusions or violates a governing requirement.
- Moderate: affects transparency, reproducibility, or a non-dominant result.
- Minor: editorial or low-influence issue.

## Closure
