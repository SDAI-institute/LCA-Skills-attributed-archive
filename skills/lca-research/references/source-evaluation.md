# Source evaluation rubric

Score or narratively assess each candidate source.

## Dimensions

- **Authority:** issuer expertise, official status, peer review.
- **Directness:** supports the exact parameter or methodological claim.
- **Transparency:** boundary, methods, raw basis, equations, assumptions.
- **Technological fit:** chemistry, route, scale, equipment, product quality.
- **Geographic fit:** country/region, grid, regulation, climate, trade.
- **Temporal fit:** data year, operating period, future applicability.
- **Completeness:** relevant flows and life-cycle stages included.
- **Precision/uncertainty:** sample size, variability, measurement/model error.
- **Consistency:** units, HHV/LHV, wet/dry basis, allocation, carbon conventions.
- **Independence/bias:** commercial incentives, conflicts, corroboration.
- **License/access:** legal right to use, transform, redistribute, or quote.

## Decision labels

- `ACCEPT_BASELINE`
- `ACCEPT_RANGE_ONLY`
- `ACCEPT_PROXY_WITH_ADJUSTMENT`
- `BACKGROUND_CONTEXT_ONLY`
- `REJECT_INCOMPATIBLE_BOUNDARY`
- `REJECT_UNVERIFIABLE`
- `REJECT_LICENSE`
- `NEEDS_EXPERT_REVIEW`

Record why rejected sources were rejected. This prevents later reuse of a convenient but incompatible number.
