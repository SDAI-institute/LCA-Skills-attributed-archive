# Evidence hierarchy

Use the highest-authority source that answers the exact question.

## Method and requirement hierarchy

1. Applicable law, regulator, program operator, current PCR/PEFCR, or contractual rule.
2. Current official standard page and legally obtained standard text.
3. Official method developer, government laboratory, database, or software documentation.
4. Peer-reviewed primary method paper or technical report.
5. Recognized guidance/review.
6. Expert judgment, clearly labeled.

## Technical data hierarchy

1. Metered site/supplier data with defined basis and QA.
2. Equipment/process design data, mass/energy balances, permits, and test reports.
3. Official statistics or sector datasets matching technology/geography/time.
4. Peer-reviewed primary studies with transparent inventories.
5. Commercial/public LCI datasets with valid license and metadata.
6. Analogous proxy adjusted transparently.
7. Expert judgment with sensitivity range.

Authority does not compensate for poor representativeness. A regulator document may define a rule but not the actual plant energy use; a plant value may be precise but not representative of future commercial operation.

Never use a search-result snippet, unsourced aggregator, marketing page, or AI-generated citation as final evidence.
