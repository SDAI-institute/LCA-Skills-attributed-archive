# LCIA and interpretation audit

Check:

- method implementation/version/category units;
- inventory-flow mapping and unmapped flows;
- GWP basis and carbon-flow treatment;
- water/land/toxicity interpretation;
- normalization/weighting disclosure;
- contribution analysis sign/cutoff;
- scenario and sensitivity coverage;
- uncertainty method, correlations, and scope;
- completeness/consistency checks;
- triangulation and order-of-magnitude plausibility;
- conclusion stability and burden shifting;
- differences vs uncertainty and decision thresholds.

Red flag any statement treating potential impacts as actual site-specific damage, health risk, or guaranteed causal consequence without supporting method/context.
