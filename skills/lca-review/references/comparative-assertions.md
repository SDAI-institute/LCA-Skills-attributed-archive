# Comparative assertions disclosed to the public

Treat as high risk.

## Minimum controls

- equivalent functions, quality, performance, lifetime, and use;
- consistent boundaries, data quality, allocation/recycling, databases/system models, LCIA methods, and assumptions;
- complete impact-category set appropriate to potential trade-offs;
- explicit uncertainty and sensitivity of ranking;
- transparent limitations and value choices;
- independent critical review by the required reviewer/panel structure;
- accessible review statement and response process.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- comparing EPDs merely because both are labeled EPD;
- comparing carbon footprints as total environmental superiority;
- using different data years/grid mixes/PCRs without harmonization;
- weighting/single score as sole public basis where rules constrain it;
- claiming significance from tiny differences without uncertainty;
- allowing the commissioner/model builder to simulate independence.
