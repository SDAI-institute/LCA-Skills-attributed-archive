# Review plan

Define before detailed review:

- study goal, audience, disclosure, comparison, program/PCR;
- applicable standards/guidance and editions;
- reviewer competence needed by sector, methods, data, LCIA, software;
- independence and conflicts;
- review scope: protocol, model, data, calculation, report, claims;
- review timing: concurrent vs final;
- access to confidential/licensed data;
- findings severity, response, closure, and appeal process;
- deliverables and public review statement.

Public comparative assertions require heightened review planning and may require a panel. Confirm current applicable requirements from authorized standards/program rules rather than relying on this repository summary.
