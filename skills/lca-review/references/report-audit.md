# Report and reproducibility audit

## Report-model agreement

Cross-check all headline and table values, units, signs, scenario names, percentages, figures, and contribution totals against frozen outputs.

## Transparency

Confirm disclosure of goal/scope, methods, data quality, versions, allocation/recycling, cutoffs, exclusions, limitations, uncertainty, review status, and prohibited uses.

## Reproducibility

Confirm availability of:

- tool/environment/version manifest;
- database/system-model/method identifiers;
- parameter/scenario files;
- model or rebuild instructions within license constraints;
- scripts/notebooks and execution order;
- raw-to-processed result lineage;
- checksums/change log.

## Claim and visual audit

Check that chart axes/units/baselines are visible, trade-offs are not hidden, net credits are decomposed, and marketing language does not exceed evidence.
