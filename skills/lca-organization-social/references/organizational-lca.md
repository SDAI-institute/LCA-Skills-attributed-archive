# Organizational LCA

Organizational LCA applies life-cycle thinking to an organization or portfolio. Use the current applicable organizational-LCA or OEF rules rather than treating a corporate GHG inventory as a full LCA.

## Boundary design

Define organization, control/consolidation approach, sites/entities, reporting period, product/service portfolio, reporting flow, value chain, capital goods, leased assets, franchises, investments where applicable, and exclusions. Record acquisitions, divestitures, outsourcing, and structural changes.

## Portfolio modeling

- Map product systems to organizational units and eliminate internal transfers.
- Allocate shared utilities, buildings, travel, IT, R&D, and overhead using causal drivers where possible.
- Preserve product-level results before aggregation.
- Normalize trends using both absolute results and relevant activity/service measures.
- Prevent double counting when the same background purchase appears in site and product models.

## Reconciliation with corporate GHG accounting

Create a bridge by gas/category/source, but preserve differences in purpose, consolidation, electricity rules, capital goods, biogenic carbon, avoided emissions, offsets, recycling, time horizon, and non-climate impacts. Do not force numerical equality by hiding methodological differences.

## Interpretation

Distinguish portfolio composition, operational efficiency, supplier change, product redesign, and organizational boundary effects. A lower intensity with higher absolute impact must be reported honestly.
