# Organizational and Social Life-Cycle Studies

Environmental product LCA, organizational LCA, corporate GHG inventories, and social LCA answer different questions. Integrate them deliberately; do not collapse them into one score.

## Organizational LCA

Read `references/organizational-lca.md`.

1. Define reporting organization, consolidation/ownership logic, reporting period, product portfolio, sites, and value-chain boundary.
2. Define a reporting flow or portfolio basis that permits comparison over time.
3. Prevent double counting across products, shared services, capital goods, and internal transfers.
4. Align environmental interventions and data quality across organizational units.
5. Reconcile—but do not equate—O-LCA results with corporate Scope 1/2/3 inventories.
6. Record organizational changes, acquisitions, divestitures, outsourcing, and base-year recalculation policy.

## Social LCA

Read `references/social-lca.md`.

1. Define stakeholder groups, social topics/indicators, geography, activity variables, and intended decision.
2. Distinguish risk/hotspot screening from site-specific performance assessment.
3. Preserve qualitative evidence and affected-stakeholder context; do not imply that a weighted social score is objective truth.
4. Address human rights, labor, community, consumer, value-chain, and governance topics with appropriate subject-matter expertise.
5. Protect personal/sensitive data and avoid unsupported allegations.

## Integration rule

Report environmental, climate, economic, and social results in separate dimensions unless a transparent multi-criteria method is explicitly chosen. Weighting across dimensions is a value choice.

## Deliverable

Produce a method-selection memo, boundary reconciliation, indicator/data plan, double-counting controls, ethical/data-protection plan, and limitations. Route formal claims to qualified domain reviewers.
