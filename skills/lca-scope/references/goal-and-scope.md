# Goal and scope protocol

## Goal statement fields

- study title/version/date;
- commissioner, practitioner, funding and conflicts;
- intended application and decision;
- reasons for carrying out the study;
- intended audience and access level;
- comparative assertion and public-disclosure status;
- program/PCR/regulatory context;
- review/verification plan;
- limitations and prohibited uses.

A useful goal describes a decision such as technology selection, product redesign, procurement, R&D prioritization, policy response, supplier engagement, reporting, or declaration—not merely “calculate environmental impacts.”

## Scope fields

- product system and functions;
- functional unit and reference flow;
- boundaries and life-cycle stages;
- geography, time, technology, operating state, lifetime;
- modeling approach and system model;
- allocation/substitution/recycling rules;
- cutoff and completeness plan;
- data quality requirements and source hierarchy;
- impact categories/method selection criteria;
- normalization/weighting status;
- interpretation, sensitivity, uncertainty, and review plan;
- report format and data confidentiality.

## Iteration rule

Scope may change when data or model behavior reveals a material issue. Every change requires a dated decision-log entry, rationale, effect on comparability, and revalidation of prior gates.
