# Modeling approach selection

## Attributional LCA

Use to describe shares of burdens associated with a product/system under a defined accounting framework, benchmark current systems, support many product footprints/EPDs, or identify hotspots. Select an internally consistent allocation/system model.

## Consequential LCA

Use to estimate environmental consequences of a decision. Define counterfactual, affected markets, marginal suppliers, constraints, scale, time horizon, substitutions, rebound, and indirect effects. Consequential logic applies across the model, not only at co-products.
