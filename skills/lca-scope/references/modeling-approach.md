# Modeling approach selection

## Attributional LCA

Use to describe shares of burdens associated with a product/system under a defined accounting framework, benchmark current systems, support many product footprints/EPDs, or identify hotspots. Select an internally consistent allocation/system model.

## Consequential LCA

Use to estimate environmental consequences of a decision. Define counterfactual, affected markets, marginal suppliers, constraints, scale, time horizon, substitutions, rebound, and indirect effects. Consequential logic applies across the model, not only at co-products.

## Prospective/ex-ante LCA

Use for future or emerging technology. Define future year, deployment scale, technology maturity, learning/scale assumptions, incumbent evolution, and background scenarios. Can be attributional or consequential.

## Dynamic/temporal LCA

Use when timing of emissions, removals, energy mix, replacements, degradation, or impacts matters. Preserve time-resolved inventory and choose compatible characterization.

## Spatial/regional LCA

Use when location materially changes electricity, water scarcity, land, toxicity/fate, transport, or ecosystem effects. Preserve geographic tags and regionalized factors.

## Hybrid LCA

Combine process and EEIO data to address truncation or organizational coverage. Define interface, price year/currency, double-counting controls, sector mapping, and uncertainty.

## Selection test

Ask whether the study is describing an existing system, comparing accounting profiles, or predicting the response to a decision. Record why rejected approaches are not fit for the intended use.
