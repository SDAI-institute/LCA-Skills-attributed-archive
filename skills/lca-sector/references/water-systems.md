# Water supply, treatment, wastewater, and desalination

## Functional unit

Use m3 of water delivered or wastewater treated at specified inlet/outlet quality, reliability, pressure, location, and receiving environment. Quality and treatment performance are part of the function.

## Inventory

Abstraction, conveyance, pumping head/efficiency, treatment chemicals, membranes/media, electricity/heat, infrastructure where material, distribution loss, sludge, backwash, concentrate/brine, disinfection by-products, leakage, wastewater collection, aeration, nutrient removal/recovery, biogas, fugitive CH4/N2O, effluent emissions, and discharge location.

## Water accounting

Distinguish withdrawal, consumption/depletion, return flow, source type, basin, season, and quality. A cubic meter is not globally equivalent. Apply scarcity or deprivation characterization only with compatible spatial/temporal data.

## Checks

- hydraulic balance: abstraction = delivered + losses + residuals/return;
- contaminant mass: influent = effluent + sludge + air emissions + transformation;
- pump energy is consistent with flow, head, efficiency, and time;
- membrane recovery and concentrate volume/solute balance close;
- recovered energy/material credits reflect usable quantity and displaced product;
- wastewater treatment does not erase upstream pollutant generation; it transfers/transforms burdens;
- negative emissions from biogas require methane leakage and counterfactual treatment.
