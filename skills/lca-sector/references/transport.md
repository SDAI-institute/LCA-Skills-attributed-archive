# Transport and logistics

## Functional unit

Use passenger-km, tonne-km, vehicle-km, shipment, or delivered product, with occupancy/load factor, distance, route, payload, backhaul, speed, vehicle/vessel class, fuel/energy basis, and service quality.

## Model components

Vehicle/vessel/aircraft production and replacement; infrastructure when material; fuel or electricity production; charging/refueling; operation; non-exhaust emissions; refrigeration; maintenance; tires/batteries; empty trips; warehousing/terminals; losses/damage; and end of life.

## Mode-specific cautions

- Road: real-world fuel economy, payload, congestion, grade, cold starts, idling, tire/brake/road wear.
- Rail: traction mix, train length, load factor, empty repositioning, network electricity.
- Marine: vessel class, speed, fuel, load factor, port operations, methane slip for gas fuels, black carbon where relevant.
- Aviation: aircraft/route, seating/freight allocation, load factor, fuel cycle, non-CO2 climate effects reported separately when included.
- EVs: charging losses, grid timing/location, battery lifetime and replacement, vehicle efficiency by climate/duty.

## Checks

Distance × payload/occupancy must reproduce transport work. Avoid defaulting every shipment to a generic fully loaded vehicle. Do not combine tailpipe-only and full-fuel-cycle results. State whether well-to-tank, tank-to-wheel, or well-to-wheel boundaries are used.
