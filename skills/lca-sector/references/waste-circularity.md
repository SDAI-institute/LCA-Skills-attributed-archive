# Waste, recycling, recovery, and circular systems

## Define the function

Clarify whether the system provides waste treatment, recovered material, product service, or all three. Define waste composition, contamination, moisture, collection geography, treatment standard, recovered-product quality, and final fate.

## Model flows

Collection, sorting, preprocessing, transport, treatment energy/materials, rejects, emissions, residues, recycling yield, quality loss, reuse/refurbishment, anaerobic digestion, composting, incineration/energy recovery, landfill gas/leachate, exported waste, and final disposal.

## Recycling conventions

Choose and document recycled-content/cut-off, end-of-life/avoided-burden, closed-loop approximation, shared/50:50, PEF circular-footprint formula, or PCR-specific treatment. Report gross burdens, incoming recycled material, outgoing secondary material, quality factors, substitution ratios, and credits separately.

## Checks

- mass closure across recovered outputs, rejects, moisture, emissions, and residues;
- recovery rate differs from recycling yield and market substitution;
- quality and demand constrain displacement;
- secondary material cannot receive both burden-free input and full virgin displacement without the chosen formula supporting it;
- energy recovery uses net exported energy and the actual displaced marginal mix;
- landfill carbon storage, methane oxidation/capture, and time horizon are explicit;
- circularity metrics do not replace environmental impact assessment;
- informal-sector and exported-waste fates are not assumed benign.
