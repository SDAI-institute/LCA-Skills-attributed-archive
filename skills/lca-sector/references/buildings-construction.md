# Buildings, construction products, and infrastructure

Follow the applicable program/PCR; construction reporting is highly module- and scenario-dependent.

## Functional and declared units

For products, record declared unit and technical performance. For whole buildings/infrastructure, use delivered service over a reference study period: floor area with use/occupancy and performance, road lane-km under traffic, bridge service, water conveyed, etc.

## Modules and parameters

Include product manufacture, transport, construction installation and loss, use/maintenance/repair/replacement/refurbishment, operational energy/water, demolition, transport, waste processing, disposal, and benefits/loads beyond the system boundary where permitted. Record reference service life, building lifetime, replacement cycles, climate zone, occupancy, energy model, grid trajectory, and end-of-life scenarios.

## Checks

- modules are not silently omitted or combined inconsistently;
- construction loss generates both extra production and waste treatment;
- replacement count handles boundary years consistently;
- operational energy model and LCA area/occupancy basis match;
- biogenic carbon and carbonation timing follow program rules;
- Module D or analogous benefits are reported separately and not netted into A–C unless required;
- EPDs used as inputs match geography, product, PCR, units, validity, and modules;
- EPDs are not assumed comparable solely because both are verified.
