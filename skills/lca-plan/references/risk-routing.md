# Risk routing

Add specialist review when the plan includes:

- multifunctionality, substitution, recycling credits, negative emissions, or storage;
- biogenic carbon, land-use change, agriculture, soil carbon, or biodiversity;
- toxicity, water scarcity, regionalized impacts, or custom characterization;
- marginal/consequential systems, prospective databases, temporal/dynamic methods;
- emerging-technology scale-up, low-TRL data, or process simulation;
- public comparison, EPD/PEF/program declaration, regulated or financial use;
- database mixing, custom mappings, API automation, or cross-tool reconciliation.

Risk routing changes evidence and review depth, not only wording.
