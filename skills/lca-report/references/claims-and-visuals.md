# Claim-safe communication and visuals

## Language

Prefer:

- “estimated potential impact under the stated assumptions”;
- “lower modeled climate-change result in the baseline scenario”;
- “hotspot within the modeled boundary”;
- “ranking is conditional on electricity/recycling assumptions.”

Avoid unsupported:

- green/eco-friendly/sustainable;
- zero-impact/zero-carbon;
- scientifically proven superior;
- verified/certified/compliant;
- actual health damage or site risk from generic LCIA.

## Visuals

- show units and functional basis;
- separate positive burdens and credits;
- show uncertainty/scenario ranges;
- avoid 3D charts and truncated axes;
- keep category units separate unless normalized/weighted transparently;
- label exclusions and “other” contribution coverage;
- provide data table or accessible alternative.

## Percentages

State baseline and absolute values. Avoid percentage changes when baseline is near zero or signs cross zero; use absolute difference and explanation instead.
