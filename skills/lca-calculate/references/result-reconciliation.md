# Result reconciliation

For each run:

1. Confirm demand/reference amount and units.
2. Reconcile total impact with summed process/flow contributions within numerical tolerance.
3. Inspect unmapped elementary flows and characterized-flow coverage.
4. Compare inventory quantities with engineering balances and expected direct emissions.
5. Compare a small known case with GUI/manual/analytical output.
6. Compare cross-tool results only after harmonizing functional unit, boundary, databases/system models, allocation, parameters, method implementation, and flow mapping.
7. Explain residual differences; do not force equality by editing factors or signs.
