# Autopilot stop conditions

Stop and return a decision packet when:

- two plausible functional units or boundaries imply materially different studies;
- a required licensed source, model, PCR, method, or supplier record is unavailable;
- a public claim requires independent review or verification;
- conservation, identity, linkage, mapping, or numerical failures remain unresolved;
- tool execution would write to a production/shared database without explicit authorization;
- confidential data would leave the approved boundary;
- results are too unstable for the intended decision;
- external publishing, submission, payment, account creation, or legal acceptance is required.
