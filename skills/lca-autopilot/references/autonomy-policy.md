# Autonomy policy

Autopilot may:

- create/update local study files within the authorized workspace;
- run non-destructive validators, local calculations, and read-only queries;
- iterate local corrections and reports;
- spawn bounded specialist reviews where supported.

Autopilot may not without a separate explicit action:

- publish, submit, email, upload, or make a public claim;
- certify, verify, sign, or impersonate an independent reviewer;
- purchase or redistribute licensed data;
- mutate shared/production databases;
- reveal confidential data;
- accept legal terms or make financial commitments.
