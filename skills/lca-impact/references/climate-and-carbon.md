# Climate, biogenic carbon, storage, and removals

## GWP basis

Record gas list, metric, time horizon, IPCC assessment report or program factor set, climate-carbon feedback treatment where relevant, and whether fossil/biogenic flows are separately characterized.

## Carbon ledger

Track fossil, biogenic, and atmospheric carbon through uptake, feedstock, products, co-products, energy, waste, emissions, storage, and end of life. Avoid both omitting uptake and crediting it twice.

## Biogenic carbon

Do not assume automatic neutrality. Evaluate:

- biomass regrowth and counterfactual land use;
- direct/indirect land-use change;
- timing of uptake/release;
- harvest/residue effects;
- product storage and end-of-life fate;
- methane/nitrous oxide and non-CO2 effects;
- method/PCR rules.

## Carbon capture and storage/use

Separate capture rate, net removal, upstream energy/materials, compression/transport, leakage, permanence, product lifetime, displaced product, and end-of-life release. A captured tonne is not necessarily an avoided or removed tonne.

## Offsets and avoided emissions

Keep outside product-system inventory unless the applicable standard/program explicitly specifies treatment. Report separately with additionality, baseline, leakage, permanence, ownership, and verification.

## Delayed emissions

Use dynamic treatment only when goal/method supports it; otherwise disclose storage duration and conventional characterization rule. Do not convert temporary storage into permanent negative emissions.
