# Climate, biogenic carbon, storage, and removals

## GWP basis

Record gas list, metric, time horizon, IPCC assessment report or program factor set, climate-carbon feedback treatment where relevant, and whether fossil/biogenic flows are separately characterized.

## Carbon ledger

Track fossil, biogenic, and atmospheric carbon through uptake, feedstock, products, co-products, energy, waste, emissions, storage, and end of life. Avoid both omitting uptake and crediting it twice.
