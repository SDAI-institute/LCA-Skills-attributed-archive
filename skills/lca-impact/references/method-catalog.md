# Major LCIA method families

## TRACI

U.S. EPA method for potential impacts such as ozone depletion, acidification, eutrophication, smog, human health, ecotoxicity, and resources depending on release/implementation. EPA currently publishes TRACI 2.2 site-generic factors; software libraries may contain older or modified implementations.

## ReCiPe 2016

Global method with midpoint and endpoint indicators and cultural perspectives. Record version, perspective, and implementation; endpoint aggregation adds modeling/value choices.

## Environmental Footprint

EU method prescribed for PEF/OEF contexts with required categories, normalization, weighting, data-quality and program rules. Use the current Recommendation, method package, and valid category/sector rules.
