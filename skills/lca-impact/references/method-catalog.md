# Major LCIA method families

## TRACI

U.S. EPA method for potential impacts such as ozone depletion, acidification, eutrophication, smog, human health, ecotoxicity, and resources depending on release/implementation. EPA currently publishes TRACI 2.2 site-generic factors; software libraries may contain older or modified implementations.

## ReCiPe 2016

Global method with midpoint and endpoint indicators and cultural perspectives. Record version, perspective, and implementation; endpoint aggregation adds modeling/value choices.

## Environmental Footprint

EU method prescribed for PEF/OEF contexts with required categories, normalization, weighting, data-quality and program rules. Use the current Recommendation, method package, and valid category/sector rules.

## IPCC climate factors

Characterize greenhouse gases by specified metric and time horizon. Record assessment report, metric (e.g., GWP100), fossil/biogenic/land-use treatment, feedback choices where relevant, and program overrides.

## CML / ILCD / EF-derived methods

Established midpoint frameworks and European recommendations with version-specific implementations. Do not assume two software packages implement identical factors.

## IMPACT World+ and regionalized methods

Can provide global/regional characterization across multiple categories; verify release, spatial resolution, and database mapping.

## USEtox

Consensus toxicity model for human toxicity and freshwater ecotoxicity. Distinguish recommended and interim factors and avoid risk-assessment interpretation.

## AWARE

Water scarcity characterization based on available water remaining; requires consumptive water use and location.

Always inspect the installed method metadata and factor list.
