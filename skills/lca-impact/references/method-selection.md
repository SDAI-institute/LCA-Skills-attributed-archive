# LCIA method selection algorithm

1. Identify mandatory program/PCR/regulatory method and version.
2. Identify geography and decision context.
3. List potentially material mechanisms from the inventory/sector.
4. Decide midpoint vs endpoint and whether regionalization is required.
5. Review elementary-flow compatibility with the database/tool.
6. Select one primary method implementation; use alternate methods for robustness, not cherry-picking.
7. Record method limitations and categories omitted.

## Typical routing

- U.S. product/process studies: current TRACI implementation may be appropriate for North American midpoint categories; verify version and climate factors.
- European PEF/program studies: use the required Environmental Footprint method and rules.
- Global/general midpoint studies: ReCiPe, CML, IMPACT World+ or other established methods may be appropriate depending on goal and implementation.
- Climate-only footprint: current required IPCC factors or program-specific factors; state assessment report and horizon.
- Toxicity: USEtox-based categories where implemented, with high uncertainty cautions.
- Water scarcity: AWARE or program-required regional method with consumption and geography.
- Construction EPD: program/PCR/EN 15804-required indicators and implementation.

Method choice should be justified at the category and implementation level, not by brand recognition.
