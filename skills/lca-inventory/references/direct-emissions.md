# Direct emissions and resource accounting

## Preferred derivation order

1. validated measurements over representative operation;
2. material/element balance;
3. stoichiometric/process model;
4. official technology-specific emission factor;
5. peer-reviewed factor/model;
6. justified proxy.

## Combustion

Track fuel composition and energy basis, oxidation, biogenic/fossil carbon, CH4/N2O/criteria pollutants, controls, startup, and flaring. Avoid applying both carbon balance and a CO2 factor for the same carbon.

## Fugitive and vented emissions

Include leak rates, pneumatic devices, storage/working/breathing losses, loading, solvent evaporation, refrigerants, methane leakage, process vents, blowdown, and control destruction/removal efficiency.

## Wastewater

Track water volume and pollutants/loads (e.g., COD, nitrogen, phosphorus, metals, organics), treatment efficiency, sludge, methane/nitrous oxide where applicable, discharge compartment, and receiving geography.

## Resource flows

Distinguish water withdrawal vs consumption and source type; land occupation vs transformation; mineral/resource extraction; renewable/nonrenewable energy carriers; and atmospheric inputs such as CO2, N2, or O2.

## Controls

A removal efficiency transfers mass; it does not make it disappear. Account for captured material fate, reagent/energy use, secondary waste, breakthrough, and residual emissions.
