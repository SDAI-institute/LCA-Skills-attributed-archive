# Mass, energy, carbon, and elemental balances

## General balance

For a defined control volume and period:

`inputs + generation - outputs - consumption = accumulation`

At steady state, accumulation is zero. Define whether each stream is wet/dry, total/component, mass/molar, and actual/standard volume.

## Mass closure

Calculate:

`closure = outputs / inputs`

and residual:

`residual = inputs - outputs`

Set a tolerance appropriate to data quality and process. A tight numerical closure produced by forcing an “unknown loss” is not evidence of correctness; identify the physical fate.

## Elemental carbon

Track carbon in feedstocks, atmospheric CO2, products, co-products, wastewater, residues, combustion gases, fugitives, stored carbon, and biogenic/fossil origin. Carbon credit/removal claims require this ledger.

## Energy

Separate electricity, shaft work, steam levels, sensible/latent heat, fuels, and exported energy. Record LHV/HHV, boiler/CHP efficiency, heat recovery, transmission/distribution, and useful vs primary energy.

## Reaction checks

Use stoichiometry, conversion, selectivity, and yield consistently. Confirm molar-to-mass conversions and limiting reactants. Include reaction water/oxygen/nitrogen where physical.

## Process-specific balances

Add nitrogen for fertilizer/biological systems, sulfur for fuels/refining, metals for mining/batteries/recycling, COD/BOD/nutrients for wastewater, and charge/electron balance for electrochemical systems.
