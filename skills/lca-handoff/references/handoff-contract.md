# Handoff contract

Required fields:

- study/status/gate/plan revision;
- intended decision and functional unit;
- model, scenario, tool, database, method, and run identities;
- completed and active task IDs;
- authoritative artifact list and hashes;
- decisions/assumptions/data requests/open findings by ID;
- validation and review status;
- claim and external-action restrictions;
- blocker or exact next action;
- author/time/version.

Keep the handoff concise and point to durable evidence.
