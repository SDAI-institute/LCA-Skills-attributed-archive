# Product-footprint context selection

Identify the deliverable before choosing rules.

| Context | Primary focus | Typical governing material | Key caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal product carbon footprint | Climate impact for product decisions | ISO 14040/44 plus chosen CFP rules | Not automatically suitable for public claims |
| CFP / partial CFP | Product climate footprint | ISO 14067 and applicable program rules | Report exclusions and biogenic/removal treatment |
| GHG Protocol Product inventory | Product life-cycle GHG inventory | Product Life Cycle Accounting and Reporting Standard | Organizational Scope 3 and product attribution are not identical |
| Type III EPD | Multi-indicator verified declaration | ISO 14025, program instructions, PCR | An unverified draft is not an EPD |
| Construction EPD | Modules and construction-specific rules | EN 15804, ISO 21930, PCR/program | Declared unit and reference service life can control comparability |
| EU PEF | Prescribed product environmental footprint | EC EF method and valid PEFCR | Use mandated data/method versions and hierarchy |
| Supplier PCF data exchange | Product data used downstream | Applicable sector/data-exchange rule | Preserve boundary, allocation, verification, and uncertainty metadata |

## Selection questions

1. Who will use the result, internally or publicly?
2. Is third-party verification required?
3. Is a valid PCR/PEFCR/program instruction available and current?
4. Is the study for one product, a representative product, an average, or a product family?
5. Is the unit functional or merely declared?
6. Which life-cycle modules and scenarios are mandatory?
7. Which database, LCIA method, electricity, recycling, carbon, and data-quality rules are prescribed?
8. Are comparative claims planned?

Freeze the rule set and validity date before data collection.
