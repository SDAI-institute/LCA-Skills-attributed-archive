# Database and model catalog

This is a routing catalog, not a license to assume fit.

## Broad process LCI

- **ecoinvent:** multi-sector global database with cut-off, APOS, consequential, and EN 15804-oriented system models depending on release/license. Record release and system model; restricted license.
- **Sphera/GaBi and commercial libraries:** broad industrial coverage under commercial terms; verify program/tool compatibility.
- **open government/sector datasets:** Federal LCA Commons, USLCI, European/JRC/EF resources, national databases, industry association datasets, PCR/EPD sources.

## Energy/transport/technology

- **Argonne R&D GREET:** U.S.-focused fuel, vehicle, energy, and technology pathways; versioned model and modules.
- **ElectricityLCI and other federal models:** regionally specific U.S. electricity inventories where current and applicable.

## Agriculture/food

- AGRIBALYSE and national/agricultural datasets; verify geography, allocation, land-use, field emissions, and database license.

## EEIO/hybrid

- **USEEIO:** U.S. environmentally extended input-output models.
- **EXIOBASE:** multi-regional EEIO; verify release/license and price-year/currency.

## Supplier/product sources

- primary supplier inventory;
- independently verified EPD/PCF;
- industry-average EPD/dataset;
- technical/environmental permits and public emission data.

An EPD impact result cannot be inserted as a unit-process inventory without category/method and boundary reconciliation.
