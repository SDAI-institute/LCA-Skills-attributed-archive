# Future-oriented approach selection

Select the method from the decision question, not from the available software.

| Question type | Primary approach | Required counterfactual | Typical output |
|---|---|---|---|
| What is the expected footprint of a future product system? | Prospective attributional LCA | Future average system | Scenario-conditioned product footprint |
| What changes because a decision or policy is implemented? | Consequential LCA | No-action or alternative decision | Net environmental consequence |
| Can an emerging technology outperform an incumbent at commercial scale? | Ex-ante LCA | Functionally equivalent mature incumbent in the same future | Performance threshold and scenario map |
| How does timing alter impacts? | Dynamic/temporal LCA | Timing-aware baseline | Time-resolved inventory or impact profile |
| Where do impacts occur and who/what is exposed? | Spatially differentiated LCA | Location-specific baseline | Region- or grid-cell-resolved result |
| How do macroeconomic supply-chain effects alter a process LCA? | Hybrid process/EEIO LCA | Consistent price-year and sector mapping | Truncation-reduced result with uncertainty |

## Classification questions

1. Is the intended conclusion descriptive or causal?
2. Is the foreground technology commercial, pilot, laboratory, or conceptual?
3. What decision year, operation years, geography, adoption scale, and market penetration apply?
4. Which parameters are forecasts and which are measurements?
5. Does background technology evolve independently of the decision?
6. Are emission timing, service life, storage, or delayed release material?
7. Is spatial differentiation decision-relevant?

## Non-equivalences

- Future attributional modeling does not become consequential merely by using a future database.
- Consequential modeling is not created by adding generic avoided-burden credits.
- Ex-ante LCA is not a license to use best-case laboratory performance as commercial performance.
- Dynamic inventory and dynamic characterization are separate choices.
- Scenario uncertainty is not a probability distribution unless probabilities are defensible.

Document the selected approach, rejected alternatives, and implications for interpretation.
