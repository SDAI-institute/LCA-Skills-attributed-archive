# GREET model fit and boundaries

R&D GREET is a versioned Argonne life-cycle model for energy and environmental effects of transportation fuels/vehicles and other end-use/technology systems.

## Common boundaries

- feedstock/fuel cycle: well-to-pump (WTP);
- vehicle operation plus fuel cycle: well-to-wheels (WTW), with WTP and pump/tank-to-wheels distinctions;
- vehicle/material production and disposal where included: cradle-to-grave;
