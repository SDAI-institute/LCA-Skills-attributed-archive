# GREET model fit and boundaries

R&D GREET is a versioned Argonne life-cycle model for energy and environmental effects of transportation fuels/vehicles and other end-use/technology systems.

## Common boundaries

- feedstock/fuel cycle: well-to-pump (WTP);
- vehicle operation plus fuel cycle: well-to-wheels (WTW), with WTP and pump/tank-to-wheels distinctions;
- vehicle/material production and disposal where included: cradle-to-grave;
- module-specific boundaries for buildings, chemicals, batteries, hydrogen, aviation, marine, etc.

Do not compare a WTP intensity with a cradle-to-grave product result without completing or aligning boundaries.

## Indicators

GREET emphasizes energy use, greenhouse gases, and regulated emissions, with coverage depending on platform/module/release. It is not a drop-in replacement for a full multi-category LCIA unless the study intentionally uses its indicator set.

## Geography

Defaults are often U.S.-oriented. Modify geography only with defensible data and document which upstream assumptions remain U.S. defaults.
