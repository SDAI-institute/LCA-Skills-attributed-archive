# Completeness, sensitivity, and consistency checks

## Completeness

- Are all inventory categories and life-cycle stages required by scope represented?
- Are omitted flows screened for cumulative and low-mass/high-impact significance?
- Are characterization factors available for material elementary flows?
- Are direct emissions, infrastructure, controls, losses, and end of life complete?

## Sensitivity

- Do conclusions survive plausible data, method, allocation, recycling, use, and boundary choices?
- Which assumptions can reverse ranking?
- What data improvements reduce decision uncertainty most?

## Consistency

- Are methods/data applied uniformly across alternatives and stages?
- Are geography/time/technology/system model and LCIA versions aligned?
- Are functional performance, lifetime, quality, and replacement consistent?
- Are credits/burdens symmetric?

## Final conclusions

Each conclusion should link to a result, significant issue, robustness evidence, limitation, and intended use. Recommendations must not exceed the study’s decision context.
