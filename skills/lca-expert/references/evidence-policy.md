# Evidence, epistemics, and source policy

## Statement classes

Tag important statements internally as:

- `SOURCE_FACT` — directly supported by an identified source;
- `MODEL_RESULT` — generated by the frozen model;
- `CALCULATION` — transparent arithmetic from stated inputs;
- `INFERENCE` — reasoned interpretation from evidence;
- `ASSUMPTION` — provisional choice;
- `SCENARIO` — deliberately constructed future/counterfactual;
- `VALUE_CHOICE` — weighting, preference, threshold, or normative judgment.

Never present an assumption or scenario as observed fact.
