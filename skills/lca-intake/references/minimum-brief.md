# Minimum intake brief

Capture:

- study ID/title, owner, commissioner, decision, audience, intended use;
- study class and risk tier;
- product/service and preliminary performance basis;
- alternatives and equivalence concerns;
- geography, period, technology, maturity, scale;
- likely boundary and modeling approach;
- required indicators/deliverables and claim route;
- primary/background data access and licenses;
- confidentiality and external-action limits;
- top assumptions, data requests, uncertainties, and review route;
- completion definition and next gate.

Label every item `confirmed`, `provisional`, `unknown-material`, or `not-applicable`.
