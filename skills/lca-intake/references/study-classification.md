# Study classification

| Class | Typical use | Minimum discipline | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explanation | Teach concepts or methods | Accurate definitions and examples | Cite current rules when material |
| Screening | Early hotspots or design directions | Transparent proxies, broad sensitivity, no external superiority claim | Upgrade before disclosure |
| Internal decision LCA | Design, procurement, investment | Frozen protocol, traceable data, QA, uncertainty-aware conclusion | Independent internal review for material decisions |
| PCF/disclosure | Product footprint or customer data | Applicable product standard/program, verification route, claim controls | Qualified verifier/program operator |
| EPD/PEF/program | Rule-governed declaration | Current PCR/PEFCR/program instructions and approved data/methods | Program verification |
| Public comparison | Public comparative assertion | Functional equivalence, symmetric rules, uncertainty, critical review | Independent panel/process |
| Consequential/prospective | Policy or technology change | Marginal suppliers, scenarios, scale-up, temporal/geographic consistency | Domain and scenario review |
| Organizational/social | Organization or social performance | Correct unit of analysis and distinct standards/data | Specialist review |
