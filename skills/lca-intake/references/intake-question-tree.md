# Intake question tree

Ask only questions whose answers can change the decision, function, boundary, model structure, evidence threshold, or claim route.

1. **Decision:** What action will change because of this study? If none, classify as educational.
2. **Audience and claim:** Internal technical team, management, customer, regulator, program operator, or public comparison?
3. **Function:** What service/performance is delivered, at what quality, lifetime, duty cycle, and conditions?
4. **Alternatives:** Are they functionally equivalent? What normalization is required?
5. **Boundary and approach:** Attributional accounting, consequential change, prospective technology, PCF/EPD/PEF/program rules, or organization/social scope?
6. **Time/geography/technology:** Reference period, production geography, market geography, technology maturity, operating scale, grid/fuel context.
7. **Data:** Primary access, supplier data, process measurements, database licenses, known gaps, confidentiality constraints.
8. **Outputs:** Indicators, scenarios, uncertainty, report format, deadline, review/verification route.
9. **Risk:** Public claim, credits, recycling, biogenic/land carbon, toxicity, water scarcity, marginal systems, novel process scale-up.

A missing answer is not automatically blocking. It is blocking when plausible alternatives can reverse the study design or conclusion and cannot be bounded with transparent scenarios.
