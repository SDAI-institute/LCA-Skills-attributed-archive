# QA checklist — Synthetic electrolytic hydrogen screening example

Record evidence paths, not only check marks. A failed material check blocks progression.

## G0 — Framing

- [ ] Intended decision, audience, disclosure status, and study type are explicit.
- [ ] Functional unit expresses quantified function/performance/time where relevant.
- [ ] Reference flows are calculated and alternatives are functionally equivalent.
- [ ] Boundary, cutoff, modeling approach, multifunctionality, and recycling rules are frozen.
- [ ] Required impact categories, review route, and program/PCR rules are identified.

## G1 — Data and model plan

- [ ] Process map and foreground/background split are complete.
- [ ] Material parameters have units, sources, representativeness, uncertainty, and model locations.
- [ ] Database/system-model compatibility and licenses are documented.
- [ ] Primary-data requests, proxy hierarchy, and sensitivity obligations are defined.
- [ ] Direct emissions and conservation checks have calculation plans.

## G2 — Inventory

- [ ] Reference products, signs, conversions, provider links, and avoided-product logic pass.
- [ ] Mass, carbon/elemental, energy, water, and yield closures pass where applicable.
- [ ] Recycle/purge, losses, controls, wastewater, catalysts, consumables, transport, use, and end of life are covered.
- [ ] Cutoff coverage and unmapped/unlinked flows are quantified.
- [ ] No duplicate recycled-content, end-of-life, export-energy, or co-product credit exists.

## G3 — LCIA

- [ ] Method family, implementation, version, indicator level, units, horizon, and factor source are frozen.
- [ ] Elementary-flow mapping and characterization coverage are quantified.
- [ ] Climate, biogenic carbon, land-use change, removals/storage, water, land, and toxicity choices are explicit.
- [ ] Regionalization and exclusions are documented.
- [ ] A benchmark/reference calculation reproduces within tolerance.

## G4 — Interpretation

- [ ] Contribution analysis traces hotspots to processes, flows, and parameters.
- [ ] Scenario, sensitivity, and uncertainty analyses address influential choices.
- [ ] Completeness, consistency, and independent benchmark checks are documented.
- [ ] Results smaller than uncertainty/model variation are not presented as decisive.
- [ ] Conclusions answer the decision and are bounded by limitations.

## G5 — Review and release

- [ ] Model, tables, charts, narrative, units, signs, scenarios, and percentages agree.
- [ ] Review findings have responses and verification evidence.
- [ ] Public comparison/EPD/PEF/PCF requirements and independent review are satisfied where required.
- [ ] Licenses, confidentiality, and claim authorization pass.
- [ ] Release manifest, file hashes, versions, data snapshot, and change history are complete.
