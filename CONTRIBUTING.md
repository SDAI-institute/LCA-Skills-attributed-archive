# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve methodological rigor, tool accuracy, sector coverage, reproducibility, or evaluation quality.

## Contribution classes

- **Methodology:** decision rules, conventions, interpretation, uncertainty, review.
- **Tooling:** openLCA, Brightway, GREET, interoperability, scripts.
- **Domain:** sector-specific inventories, performance functions, direct-emission models, red flags.
- **Evidence:** authoritative source updates and standards status.
- **Evaluation:** adversarial cases and expected senior-practitioner behavior.

## Pull-request requirements

A change should include:

1. a concise problem statement;
2. the affected skill or reference files;
3. supporting primary sources for factual or methodological claims;
4. an eval addition when behavior changes;
5. passing validation and tests;
6. confirmation that no restricted content or data is included.

## Standards and copyrighted material

Do not paste clauses, tables, annexes, or substantial excerpts from ISO, EN, PCR, database, or commercial method documents. Paraphrase at a high level and cite the official source. Contributors must have the right to share any submitted data or examples.

## Versioning

- Patch: clarifications, fixes, non-breaking source updates.
- Minor: new skills, new sector modules, new tool workflows, substantive backward-compatible behavior.
- Major: changes to study artifact contracts, routing, or quality gates that require migration.
